SkyShift

Watch the infrared sky move.

Since 2025, NASA's SPHEREx has photographed the whole sky every six months in 102 infrared colours. Enter a name or coordinates, and SkyShift lines up every SPHEREx picture of that spot and plays them like a flipbook. Anything that moves or changes brightness jumps out.
